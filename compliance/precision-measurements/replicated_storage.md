# Precision measurements: replicated_storage

Eight independent scrambled Sobol replicates per run; a float64 prefix paired within each. Mean errors use independent replicate estimates and Student t intervals (7 degrees of freedom). Matched float64 draws. Paired counts round up to whole storage blocks within each replicate.

Kind: portfolio. Market: USD, the shared portfolio's 3% -> 5% curves (tests/support/portfolio.py). Seeds: 32 (1..32). Commit b3b4ecce2f42 (local changes: yes; engine digest bb441986967f468d), JAX 0.10.2 on cpu (cpu), 2026-10-16T04:32:30+00:00.

Each figure's precision error is its value less the float64 run's on the same paths, per seed. Relative error: its root mean square over the seeds in units of the figure's statistical error (the float64 figure's spread over the seeds). Relative bias: its mean over the seeds in the same units. Significance: that mean in units of its own standard error. Each the worst over the figures and their dates. Entries float64 holds fixed (a statistical error below 1e-9 of the figure's largest value: a netting set's ENE where every path is in the money, an EEE_B at its t=0 value) are left out of the ratios; Fixed moved is the largest error on them relative to the figure's largest value. Generated by `tests/support/precision_harness.py`; the module docstring defines every quantity.
