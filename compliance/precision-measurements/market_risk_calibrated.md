# Precision measurements: market_risk_calibrated

The `market_risk` campaign's market, trades, shifts and coarse grid with the Bermudan calibrated to the market's swaptions (ORE's CoterminalDealStrike) and recalibrated under every scenario, as ORE's historical P&L recalibrates it (I-112): each scenario its own model and induction, where the `market_risk` campaigns share one fixed LGM. Each case stores the shifts (`simulation`) or each trade's P&L (`pricing`) in one format, revalued in float64; or computes each trade's matrix products in one compute format, accumulated and stored in float32 (the `float32` case is the accumulator's floor; `<format>_1x` the single pass, A-23), to nearest, and the `rounding_market_risk` formats stochastically.

Kind: market_risk. Market: USD, the market-risk tests' sloped two-curve market (tests/market_risk_support.py). Seeds: 16 (1..16). Commit 693ab3236ebc (local changes: yes; engine digest 9276035e71494e18), JAX 0.10.2 on cpu (cpu), 2026-10-18T20:17:29+00:00.

Pricing settings (the evidence table's key, I-114): `bermudan_swaption` engine=LgmGrid nx=8 sx=3 model=recalibrated; `bond` engine=Discounting; `european_swaption` engine=Bachelier; `swap` engine=Discounting.

Each figure's precision error is its value less the float64 run's on the same paths, per seed. Relative error: its root mean square over the seeds in units of the figure's statistical error (the float64 figure's spread over the seeds). Relative bias: its mean over the seeds in the same units. Significance: that mean in units of its own standard error. Each the worst over the figures and their dates. Entries float64 holds fixed (a statistical error below 1e-9 of the figure's largest value: a netting set's ENE where every path is in the money, an EEE_B at its t=0 value) are left out of the ratios; Fixed moved is the largest error on them relative to the figure's largest value. Generated by `tests/support/precision_harness.py`; the module docstring defines every quantity.

Coverage below is diagnostic Student t coverage, not available confidence bounds. Current runs report nominal intervals unavailable for every figure and entry (I-111). The per-entry audit retains the predeclared >=0.90 coverage and 0.75–1.33 standardized-error RMS criteria; unavailable or uninformative diagnostics never count as passes. A diagnostic pass does not make a nominal interval available. Historical rows without an entry label pooled dates and omitted zero-width misses.
