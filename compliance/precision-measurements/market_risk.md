# Precision measurements: market_risk

Market risk on the market-risk tests' sloped two-curve market (tests/market_risk_support.py): a 10Y payer swap and a 9Y receiver that offsets most of it, a European, a Bermudan and a bond, revalued under 10-day Monte Carlo shifts of every pillar. Each case stores the shifts (`simulation`) or each trade's P&L (`pricing`) in one format, revalued in float64.

Each figure's precision error is its value less the float64 run's on the same paths, per seed. Relative error: its root mean square over the seeds in units of the figure's statistical error (the float64 figure's spread over the seeds). Relative bias: its mean over the seeds in the same units. Significance: that mean in units of its own standard error. Each the worst over the figures and their dates. Entries float64 holds fixed (a statistical error below 1e-9 of the figure's largest value: a netting set's ENE where every path is in the money, an EEE_B at its t=0 value) are left out of the ratios; Fixed moved is the largest error on them relative to the figure's largest value.
