# Precision evidence

Generated by `python -m tests.support.precision_evidence` from the precision harness's measurements of record ([precision-measurements/](precision-measurements/)) and the acceptance standard ([precision-acceptance.json](precision-acceptance.json)); do not edit. The engine reads the same rows (`engine/precision/evidence.json`) and warns of every figure of a run that has no passing row, saying what is validated and up to how many paths ([I-55](../docs/planning/known-issues.md#i-55), decision D-9: no run is refused on precision). A passing row is engineering evidence against decision A-11's standard, not a regulatory approval.

## The acceptance standard (A-11)

| Rule | Threshold | Applies to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statistical | The root mean square precision error over the seeds, at most this share of the figure's statistical error (its float64 spread over the seeds), on every entry float64 does not hold fixed. Share: 0.01 | Every figure. For the market-risk figures (P&L, VaR, ES) it is the Basel plan's P6.2 rule; for every other figure (NPV, EPE, ENE, EE_B, EEE_B, EPE_B, EEPE_B, PFE and the Basel EPE and EEPE) an engineering rule, Basel setting no numeric tolerance for them (CRE53 requires EPE backtesting only). | Basel plan P6.2 (docs/planning/details/basel-iii.md, Phase 6), decision A-11 |
| Fixed entries | On an entry float64 holds fixed (its statistical error below 1e-9 of the figure's largest float64 value: a netting set's ENE where every path is in the money, an EEE_B or EEPE_B at its t=0 value, a matured trade's value), the root mean square precision error at most this share of the figure's largest float64 value. Share: 1e-06 | Every figure with such entries; an engineering rule. | Basel plan P6.2's rule for figures without randomness (1e-6 relative), applied to the entries float64 holds fixed (I-55, 2026-10-11): they have no statistical error for the statistical rule to take a share of |
| P&L attribution | The P&L attribution test's green zone between the reduced-precision and the float64 P&L on the same scenarios, at every seed: Spearman's rank correlation above 0.80 and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic below 0.09. | The market-risk figures (P&L, VaR, ES), with the statistical rule (A-11). MAR32 tests 250 days of a desk's P&L; here it compares two P&Ls of thousands of scenarios, where its thresholds are loose, so it is a floor. | MAR32.42(1) (green zone; MAR32.34, MAR32.38 and MAR32.41 define the two metrics); Basel Framework, MAR32, in force 2023-01-01, published 2020-03-27, https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/32.htm?inforce=20230101&published=20200327, retrieved 2026-10-11. Single entry, from the source above, 2026-10-11; it agrees with the Basel plan's orientation values (Appendix B, which is not a source). The second, independent entry is the Basel plan's P0.3 double-entry transcription, after which these values are read from the regulatory profile. |

## Reading it

- **A row** is one figure kind of one scope under one case of a campaign: a product (the worst of the campaign's trades of it) or the aggregate (the netting set, the market-risk portfolio). Its combination is the precision of every stage the figure depends on, `<stage>=<storage>/<compute>/<accumulate>`, then the rounding and block layout where a stage stores in a scaled format; its case label in the campaign's terms (`tests/support/precision_harness.py`): `<stage>:<format>[:<rounding>:<layout>]` stores one stage, `<stage>:compute:<format>` computes one stage's products in a format, `throughout:<format>` every stage, and `:paired` adds a paired float64 sample.
- **It passes at a path count** when, over the campaign's seeds, its root mean square precision error is within the statistical rule on every entry float64 does not hold fixed and within the fixed rule on every entry it does, and for market risk the P&L attribution test is green at every seed. The errors are measured across independent seeds, never from the paired estimator's own standard errors (I-111).
- **The path ceiling** is the largest measured path count at which the row passes and at every smaller one; none if it fails at the smallest. On Sobol paths the precision error grows against the statistical error with the path count ([precision.md §15.4](../docs/planning/details/precision.md#154-the-storage-harness-2026-10-09)), so a run with fewer paths than a ceiling is validated, and one with more is not.
- **A run's figure is validated** by the rows of its combination, scope and kind (`engine.precision.evidence`): a trade's by its product's rows; the aggregate's by a row whose campaign held every product of the run, with one pricing stage for all its trades; a figure the paired sample corrects (EPE, ENE, EE_B, EEE_B, EPE_B, EEPE_B, the Basel EPE and EEPE) by the rows of the largest paired share at most the run's; the smallest ceiling where rows of two campaigns match.
- **Formats below float32 were emulated** on the CPU named, which computes what native units compute ([precision.md §8.3](../docs/planning/details/precision.md#83-emulation-and-native-speed)). Each campaign's row names the code it measured: its commit, whether the tree had local changes, and the digest of the engine's Python sources.
